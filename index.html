About

Championing inclusive growth across Pakistan and beyond.

With over two decades of multi-sector experience, Azam Malik has worked with leading media organizations including Daily Jang, The Frontier Post, Daily Pakistan, and Khaleej Today. As a trainer, he has supported UNDP, UNOPS, The Asia Foundation, SOS, NRSP, PRSP, BISP, ManTech & ARC, HRLC, CHRS, and Possibilities Pvt. Ltd.

He is the Founder of Punjabica, CEO of Kitab Publications, and a Creative Strategist behind Pakistan's flagship innovation platform Future Fest.
