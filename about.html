The story behind the movement.

Professional Trainer, Motivational Speaker, Development Activist, and ecosystem architect enabling Pakistan's innovation economy through Ejad Labs, Future Fest, Digital Pakistan Movement, and advisory missions across the region.

Rooted in journalism, scaled through impact.

From early contributions to Daily Jang, The Frontier Post, Daily Pakistan, and Khaleej Today, Azam cultivated a voice for civic discourse. He later translated that experience into transformative programs for UNDP, UNOPS, The Asia Foundation, SOS, NRSP, PRSP, BISP, ManTech & ARC, HRLC, CHRS, and Possibilities Pvt. Ltd.

His academic foundation combines a Master's in Persian from GCU Lahore, an MBA from PCBI, an MPhil from AIOU, and advanced learning in Social Engineering at NISEGIS and entrepreneurship at the University of Maryland.
