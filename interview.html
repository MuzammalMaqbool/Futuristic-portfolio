Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

What inspired Future Fest?

A desire to connect Pakistan's brightest founders, artists, and policymakers on one stage, catalyzing partnerships that would usually take months into days.

How do you design impactful trainings?

Begin with empathy interviews, co-create with local stakeholders, and ensure every module ties knowledge to an actionable outcome.

Advice for young leaders?

Explore your cultural roots, stay globally curious, and build coalitions instead of silos.

What drives the Digital Pakistan Movement?

The belief that every Pakistani deserves access to digital skills and opportunities, regardless of their background or location.
